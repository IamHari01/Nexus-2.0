# **App Name**: NexusTalent AI

## Core Features:

- ATS Simulation: Simulate real-world Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to assess candidate resume fit against job descriptions using semantic matching, not just keywords.  This acts as a tool to maximize shortlisting probability, with penalties for missing hard requirements and rewards for project-level evidence.
- Real-time Job Sourcing: Fetch and filter real-time job listings based on target job title, location, and career level, removing duplicates and expired listings.
- Skills Gap Analysis: Identify specific missing skills based on ATS scoring, classifying them into hard skills, experience gaps, and tooling/workflow gaps, to explain why a candidate might be rejected.
- Personalized Learning Paths: Generate job-specific learning paths recommending YouTube channels, documentation, and mini-projects optimized for shortlisting, focusing on hands-on, recent material.
- Shortlisting Probability Score: Display a Shortlisting Probability score (0-99) reflecting ATS-grade semantic matching, experience depth, tooling familiarity, and industry context.
- Live Job Details Display: Display job details with company, title, location, match status, reasoning, matched skills, missing skills, and a direct job link.
- Resume Parsing & Upload: Allow users to upload their resume (PDF/Text) for parsing and use in the career intelligence engine.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#3F51B5) for a professional and trustworthy feel.
- Background color: Light gray (#F5F5F5) to provide a clean and modern look.
- Accent color: Teal (#009688) to highlight key actions and information.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', sans-serif, for a modern, machined, neutral look suitable for headlines or body text.
- Use clear, industry-standard icons for skills, tools, and learning resources.
- Employ a clean, structured layout with clear sections for job details, skill gaps, and learning paths.
- Subtle animations to indicate loading states and transitions between different analyses.
